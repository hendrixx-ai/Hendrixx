## 1. Three Campaign Concepts

### Concept 1 — 🔦 "PIMA" (The Test) — *recommended lead*
- **Big idea:** Make the testing moment the brand ritual. Every single asset shows the same beat: a hand switches the product ON, in front of the buyer's eyes. Lockup: **"Pima Kwanza. Lipa Baadaye."**
- **Why it works (tied to positioning):** It makes the ONE core message *visible* — and it's literally how the boss already sells. Answers the #1 fear (fakes) without saying the word. Costs nothing extra: the ritual is free content.
- **Visual signature:** a small green **✓ IMEPIMWA** tag in the corner of every image — the brand's stamp.
- **Execution risk:** repetition — counter by varying settings (kiosk, home veranda, boda stage, duka la mahindi).

### Concept 2 — 🌙 "MWANGA USIKATIKE" (The Blackout Split)
- **Big idea:** Split frame. Left: night-navy darkness, "Stima imekatika." Right: the *same* scene warm-lit by one Hendrixx Power lamp. One lamp changes the color of the night.
- **Why it works:** wordless emotion; every TZ viewer has lived the left side. Dramatizes the sourced pain point (blackouts, research §3.1–3.2).
- **Execution risk:** the hardest shot on a phone (low light) — but the lamp is the only light source you need, which *is* the shot. One night, one take.

### Concept 3 — 📄 "KARATASI" (The Paper Promise)
- **Big idea:** the **warranty card is the hero object** — macro shot, held between two fingers, "MIEZI 3" readable. "Warranty ya kweli iko karatasi."
- **Why it works:** turns the anti-fake angle into a physical artifact; kiosk/reseller buyers respect paper. Converts Concept 1's promise into something you can hold.
- **Execution risk:** the "coldest" of the three — best as a *supporting* asset, not the lead campaign.

**🎯 Recommendation: PIMA leads.** Steal one hero shot from MWANGA USIKATIKE (the emotional status graphic) and the close-up discipline of KARATASI (the trust asset). One system, three flavors.

## 2. Visual Direction (one system across all three)

- **Palette:**
  - Solar Amber `#F6A821` — light, energy, the lamp glow
  - Night Navy `#10233F` — the blackout, backgrounds
  - Check Green `#1E8E3E` — the ✓ IMEPIMWA tag, confirmation moments
  - Paper White `#FAFAF7` — cards, text fields
  - Charcoal `#202124` — body text
- **Typography:** one heavy geometric sans for headlines (Montserrat ExtraBold / Archivo Black, free in Canva), one clean sans for body (Inter / Noto Sans). Swahili is Latin script — no font issues. Headlines BIG — most views are on small phones.
- **Imagery:** **real phone photos only** — real hands, real products working, natural light. No stock photos, no rendered phones, no borrowed brand logos.
- **Motion notes:** static camera (lean the phone on something), hand enters frame, product switches on, light floods, one text overlay appears. 5–15 seconds, silent-friendly (add one-line caption).
- **Do / Don't:**
  - ✅ DO: one message per frame · WhatsApp number on every asset · green ✓ tag on every product image · Swahili-first text · real settings (duka, nyumba, barabara)
  - ❌ DON'T: fake 5-star/review badges (**no reviews exist — inventing them breaks the hard rules**) · photoshopped specs · English-only consumer assets · stock-photo families · more than 2 fonts/colors per frame

### A2 — Jiji Photo Formula · 5 shots per product · min 1080px, 4:5 or 1:1
1. **Clean front** — product on plain cloth (kanga/kitenge), window light, lens wiped, tap to focus.
2. **Hand scale shot** — product in hand so buyers judge size honestly.
3. **Action shot** — lamp **charging a phone** (cable visible, phone screen showing charge icon). This is the money shot.
4. **The dark test** — at night/covered box: lamp only light source. Proves it works.
5. **Trust shot** — product **next to the warranty card**, green ✓ IMEPIMWA tag visible.
Every shot: green ✓ tag added in edit (Canva sticker). First photo = Action shot (Jiji thumbnail must sell in 0.5 sec).
