## 0. The Honest Economic Picture First

Your total capital is **TSh 500k–2M, and it must mostly buy stock.** Ads are a minority slice. So this plan is structured **free-channels-first, paid-second**:

- **Phase 0 (free, starts the day stock lands):** WhatsApp broadcast + status, Jiji listings, Instagram organic. Costs TSh 0.
- **Phase 1 (small paid test):** ONE Meta Click-to-WhatsApp campaign + optional Jiji listing upgrade — only after prices are set and photos exist.
- **Phase 2 (scale):** only after Phase 1 produces *your real* cost-per-sale numbers.

## 5. Measurement Plan (day 1 — all manual, all real)

**The order log (most important asset — one line per sale):**
`date | channel (status / Jiji / IG / Meta ad / referral / walk-in) | item | unit cost | sell price | margin | warranty claim?`

- **WhatsApp:** count daily "MWANGA" keyword arrivals (auto-reply makes this countable); ask every buyer *"Ulitoka wapi?"* and log the answer.
- **Meta Ads Manager:** messages started + cost per message. Nothing else matters at this budget.
- **KPI definitions:** Units/week · gross margin/week · quote→sale rate (deals closed ÷ quotes given) · stock-turn days (days from stock landing → sold out).
- **UTMs:** not applicable to WhatsApp/Jiji; if a link-in-bio page ever goes live: `?utm_source=ig-bio&utm_campaign=hp-launch`.
- **Review cadence:** 2-min daily glance at messages; 30-min weekly review vs the log; judge paid tests only on 7-day windows.
- **Explicitly absent: ROAS, CTR benchmarks, 'industry averages.'** None exist here. Your log becomes the benchmark — then the Analytics Agent (step 7) reads it.

## 6. Optimization Plan (decision rules, not predictions)

- **SCALE:** a variant clearly wins on cost-per-message over 7 days → shift ~70% of daily budget to it; consider +TSh 2–3k/day only if stock can sustain it.
- **FIX (ads fine, sales not):** messages arrive but few buy → it's a price/offer/objection problem, not an ad problem. Collect the exact objections in WhatsApp, bring them back to the Copy Agent.
- **KILL:** a variant produces no message conversations after ~TSh 30–40k spend (⚠️ framework number, your call) → pause it, redistribute.
- **FREE-CHANNELS-WIN rule:** if Phase 0 sells the first stock in <2 weeks, pause paid plans and restock — cheapest capital deployment wins at this size.
- **Restock gate:** never spend on ads for stock you can't replenish — the buffer bucket exists for restocking winners, not for ad escalations.
