## 1. Positioning Statement

> **For** people in Kahama and Dar es Salaam whose work stops when the power does,
> **Hendrixx Power** is the local power-and-phone-gear seller
> **that** tests every unit in front of you, backs it with a written warranty, and takes payment only when it's in your hands —
> **because** the market is flooded with fakes that die early and vanish with the seller.

*(Name "Hendrixx Power" is a working label — boss may keep, change, or drop it.)*

## 2. ONE Core Message

> **"Tested power & phone gear with a real warranty — pay when it's in your hands."**

What it deliberately leaves out: prices (set at sourcing), range size (still being bought), speed of delivery (unproven — never promise what hasn't been done once).

## 3. Target Audience

**Primary:** Kahama & Dar earners hit by blackouts and dead batteries — households, boda riders, kiosk/small-shop owners. Trigger moment: power cut or dead phone **during a working day**, or the death of a fake unit they already bought. They buy via Jiji/Instagram/WhatsApp, pay M-Pesa or COD, and need to **see it work before they pay**.

**Secondary:** kiosk/stall owners who buy 3–10 units to resell — the small-wholesale lane.

*(Audience language: Swahili-first — copy step delivers bilingual options.)*

## 4. The Problem, in Their Words

- "Stima inakatika — simu inakufa, biashara inasimama." *(Power cuts — phone dies, business stops.)*
- "Nilinunua solar ya bei rahisi, iliharibika baada ya mwezi mmoja — hakuna warranty." *(Bought cheap solar, it broke after a month — no warranty.)*
- "Nachiwa na battery kabla ya mchana kuisha." *(Battery's dead before midday.)*
- "Siwezi kulipa kitu sijakipima." *(I can't pay for something I haven't tested.)*

*(Lines 1–3 grounded in sourced pain points — power unreliability and fake-product flood, research §4; phrasing is ours, boss should sanity-check the Swahili with real customers.)*

## 5. Unique Angle — one line vs. each alternative

| Alternative | Their weakness (sourced) | Our one line |
|---|---|---|
| Cheap market-stall solar | Fake/low-quality flood, no after-sales (energypedia, research §3.4) | "Same price band, but tested + real warranty" |
| Jumia/Kilimall | No human to hold accountable; returns friction (⚠️ ASSUMPTION — verify with local buyers) | "A person you can call on WhatsApp, not a call center" |

| Doing nothing (candles, charging kiosks) | Recurring cost + lost hours during blackouts | "Own your power once, use it every blackout" |

1. **Core:** Tested power & phone gear with a real warranty — pay when it's in your hands.
2. **Support 1 — No fakes:** every unit hand-checked before sale *(internal promise — must be operationally true: boss commits to testing each unit)*

## 8. Claims Policy

**Can say:** sourced market facts (with sources in `1-research.md`); product truths checked before each sale; COD/warranty as actually offered.

**Must never say (until proof exists):** units-sold or revenue ("hundreds sold" — we've sold zero), "cheapest/leading in TZ", warranty duration before the boss sets it, income guarantees, any performance numbers. **There is no performance data yet — if asked, we say so.**
