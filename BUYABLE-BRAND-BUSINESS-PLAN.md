**Prepared for:** a creative maker starting from zero  
**Audience:** ambitious beginners  
**Daily topic:** money, career, and building a brand  
**Brand strategy:** authority first  
**Income target:** $10,000 / month

| Dimension | You chose | What it actually means for the business |
|---|---|---|
| Skills | Creative / aesthetic craft (design, photo, video, fashion, beauty, cooking, music, writing, DIY) | Your **unfair advantage is taste**. Most money-niche creators look cheap. You will not. |

### The honest positioning

You are not “another Instagram growth guru.”

You are **the creative who teaches ambitious beginners how to turn taste into something people can buy** — a digital product, a freelance offer, a personal brand that prints inquiries.

Your content promise:

> “I will show you how to look premium, package what you know, and sell it on Instagram — without waiting to go viral and without becoming a full-time influencer.”

That sentence is the whole business.

### Honest math on $10k/month (do not skip this)

From zero, $10k in month 1 is fantasy. $10k as a **system** is realistic if you treat this like a job.

| Path to ~$10k/month | What it takes |
|---|---|
| Core offer only at **$147** | ~68 sales / month |
| Core **$147** + tripwire **$27** + upsell **$297** | ~35 core + 40 tripwire + 12 upsells |
| Later: **$497** group / review round | ~12–20 high-ticket + remaining from the ladder |

**Timeline if you post daily and sell weekly:**

- **Days 1–30:** brand + lead magnet + first product live. Goal: first 10 customers, not $10k.
- **Days 31–90:** prove the offer. Goal: $1k–$3k/month.
- **Months 4–6:** tighten content + testimonials. Goal: $3k–$6k/month.
- **Months 7–12:** launches + upsell + authority. Goal: $10k/month becomes possible.

Authority-first is slower at the start and **more valuable** later. That is the trade you chose. This plan respects it.

## Idea 1 — *First Product in a Weekend* (tripwire / starter)

**Product format**  
Short guide + Notion checklist + Canva mockup templates + 4–6 loom-style videos (total watch time under 90 minutes). Delivered as a zip / Google Drive / Stan Store file. Not a “course.” A **done-with-you kit**.

**Ideal customer**  
Ambitious beginner, 20–35, creative or “I make things,” tired of consuming content, has never sold a digital product, does not want to become an influencer.

**Pain point it solves**  
“I don’t know what to sell, I’m overwhelmed by 8-week courses, and I don’t want to show my face rambling for 6 months with nothing to buy.”

**Why people would buy it now**  
- Cost of living + job anxiety = people want a side product, not a side hustle lecture.  
- AI made “start an online business” feel urgent and confusing. Beginners want a **finite, weekend-sized** first win.  
- Cheap enough to buy on emotion after one good Reel.

**Realistic pricing**  
**$27** (standard) / **$19** launch / **$37** once you have 10 reviews.  
Do not price this at $97. It is a foot-in-the-door product.

**Easiest way to start selling it**  
1. Build v1 in 1 weekend (yes, actually).  
2. Stan Store or Gumroad checkout.  
3. Instagram bio link + one Reel per day with a comment keyword (`WEEKEND`).  
4. Sell to DMs before you even have a website.

**Revenue role in the $10k plan**  
Volume + email list + upsell into the core offer. This is **not** the $10k product. It is the customer-getting product.

## Idea 2 — *Pretty Inquiries: Instagram Client Engine for Creatives* (service offer in a box)

**Product format**  
Self-paced mini-course (8–10 short videos) + DM scripts + portfolio layout templates + “3 offer” swipe file (what to sell as a designer / photographer / editor / stylist).

**Ideal customer**  
Creative who already has *some* skill (Canva, camera, video, fashion, beauty, cooking content) and posts sometimes, but **nobody is paying them**.

**Pain point it solves**  
Pretty feed. Zero invoices. They confuse “posting” with “selling.” They undercharge or hide their services in the bio like a hobby.

**Why people would buy it now**  
- Freelance platforms are crowded and race-to-the-bottom. Instagram is still where taste gets hired.  
- Beginners would rather get **one $500 client** than learn funnels.  
- You can demonstrate this live: before/after bios, offer screenshots, mock DMs.

**Realistic pricing**  
**$97** (sweet spot) or **$127** with a 15-minute async feedback bonus.

**Easiest way to start selling it**  
Publicly rebuild 5 stranger bios/offers for free on Reels → waitlist → $97 product. Social proof before you have clients of your own.

**Revenue role**  
Strong, but it pulls you toward **freelancing education**, which can trap you in “get clients” content. Fine as a later product. Not the cleanest authority brand for “digital products.”

**Realistic pricing**

## Transformation promised

**Before:**  
You have taste, screenshots, and “ideas.” You post when you feel inspired. Your bio is a vibe. Nobody knows what you sell. You are one of 10,000 aesthetic accounts.

**After:**  
You have a **named offer**, a **premium-looking Instagram that actually sells**, a **simple funnel** (Reel → comment → DM → checkout), and a repeatable 30-minute workday. Strangers can give you money this week.

*In 14 days, install a buyable personal brand and your first digital product — without a big following and without looking like a slimy guru.*

**Mechanism (what makes it different):**  
Not “post more.” Not “find your passion.”  
The mechanism is: **Offer first, aesthetic second, audience third.**  
Most beginners do this backwards. That is why they stay broke and pretty.

**Rule:** Create once on Instagram. Cross-post the Reel and the carousel image set to Facebook the same day. Once a week, drop a longer written post into 2–3 relevant Facebook groups (no spam links on first comment — value first).

The plan below is **Meta-native**: it works as Instagram native and Facebook Reels/Feed.

## Simple sales funnel: Reel → DM → purchase

### The 60-second Reel that starts the funnel

1. **0–3s hook:** “If your Instagram is pretty and your PayPal is empty, pause this.”  
2. **3–20s problem:** Bio is a vibe. No offer. No checkout. Waiting for 10k followers.  
3. **20–40s mechanism:** Offer first, aesthetic second, audience third. Hold up the Map.  
4. **40–55s proof:** “I opened checkout at [X] followers. Here’s the page.”  
5. **55–60s command:** “Comment BRAND. I’ll send the map. Then we talk.”

### What happens next (you do this every day)

1. Auto-DM fires with the map.  
2. You reply to **SELLING / DECIDING** within a few hours (not 4 days).  
3. One clarifying question max. Then a single recommendation: 27 or 147.  
4. Send **one** link. Stop dumping PDFs.  
5. If they click and don’t buy: Story mention 12h later — “If I DMed you the map, the $147 install is the part that makes it real. Questions?”  
6. After purchase: Welcome note + “start Module 0” + ask for a screenshot of their one-sentence offer (future content, with permission).

### Conversion targets (so you know if it’s working)

| Stage | Healthy beginner benchmark |
|---|---|
| Reel views → comments | 1–3% |
| Comments → DM replies | 40%+ |
| Replies → checkout clicks | 20–40% |
| Clicks → purchases | 8–20% |
| Map downloads → $147 in 10 days | 1–3% (email + DM combined) |

If comments are high and DMs are empty, your keyword is unclear.  
If DMs are high and sales are zero, your promise is fuzzy or your price page is weak.  
If nothing is happening, you are posting diary content with no command.
