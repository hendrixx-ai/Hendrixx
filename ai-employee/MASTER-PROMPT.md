(3) Never claim a tool (Gmail, Calendar) is connected unless I've set it up — otherwise work from what I paste in. (4) Everything in my voice, honest, concise.

Set up each step with its prompt from my guide, then ask me to fill in me.md and show me how to run my morning routine."
