### The role you're paying for

You have a front-desk person. Their job is to answer the phone, book appointments, and make sure nobody walks out of Coco Plaza without a slot reserved.

The problem is they can't be there 24 hours a day. Esper runs 7 days a week, 10:00–19:30. When that person is with a client, on lunch, or gone for the day — your phone keeps ringing and nobody picks up.

### What that costs you

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Front-desk salary (fully-loaded) | ~$400–800/month |
| Calls your shop gets on a busy day | 30–50 calls/day |
| Calls missed when desk is empty | ~60–190 calls/month |
| Callers who never call back | ~85% of those |
| **Estimated monthly loss from missed calls** | **~$1,600–5,000** |

That's TZS 4–13 million in appointments that never booked. At Esper's ticket prices — Moroccan hammam TZS 250K, body scrub TZS 100K, a facial TZS 150K — that's roughly 20–60 treatments walking out the door every month. Before repeat clients and referrals.

### The fix

An AI assistant that:
- Answers Esper's phone in Esper's voice, in English or Swahili
- Books appointments straight into your calendar
- Sends confirmations and reminders (fewer no-shows)
- Covers the 7:35pm calls, the Sunday afternoon rings, and the times your desk is with a client
- Works on WhatsApp too

You don't hire anyone. You don't buy equipment. You don't change your phone number.
