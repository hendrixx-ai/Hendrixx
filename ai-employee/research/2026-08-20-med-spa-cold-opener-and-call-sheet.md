>

>
> — Hendrixx

**Word count:** 72

**Why it works for a 55-year-old owner:**
- Opens with a number they recognize ($3,200 = what they pay the desk + what missed calls cost — backed by the leak model in the vertical-comparison research)

## Prompt 4 — Discovery call questions (15-minute call)

### Opening

> *"What's your most expensive role — the person or thing that costs you the most every month?"*

**Listen for:** receptionist salary, a senior therapist, missed-call leak, or "I don't know." Whatever they say first is what they're already thinking about — use it as the hook.

### Question 2 — Call volume reality check

> *"How many calls would you say come in on a busy day? And how many ring out when the desk is with a client or after hours?"*

**Listen for:** "10–15" (small), "20–30" (medium), "40+" (busy — your target).
**If they say "I don't track it":** → *"That's common. I can run a free audit on your line for 3 days — you'll see every missed ring in black and white."*

### Question 3 — The after-hours gap

> *"What happens when a customer calls at 8pm on a Saturday — does anyone pick up?"*

**Listen for:** "Goes to voicemail" (leak ✓), "I answer on my personal phone" (owner burnout ✓), "We have a staff phone that gets forwarded" (partial solution).
**If they say "voicemail is fine":** → *"About 80% of people who reach voicemail hang up without leaving a message, and 85% don't call back. So that 8pm caller — she's calling your competitor next."*

### Question 4 — Booking process & no-shows

> *"Walk me through how a booking happens right now — from the call to the chair."*

**Listen for:** paper book, Google Calendar, Fresha/online, WhatsApp. Whatever system they use is what your AI needs to plug into.
**If they say "it's just me and WhatsApp":** → *"So you're doing the bookings yourself, after hours, on your personal phone. What's that costing you in time? Rough guess — 5, 10, 15 hours a week?"*

### Question 5 — The close

> *"If I could show you a way to answer every call, book every slot, and cut no-shows in half — for less than what you're spending on missed calls alone — would you give it a shot for a month?"*

**Listen for:** "Yes" → book the pilot. "How much?" → *"Flat $2,500/mo. No setup fee, no contract. If it doesn't pay for itself in month one, cancel."* "I need to think about it" → *"Fair. What's the one thing that gives you pause?"* then handle below.

### Common objections & hesitation points — prepared responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| **"My receptionist costs less than that."** | "She costs $400–800/mo. But she's not there at 8pm, on Sunday, or when she's with a client. The AI handles those gaps — and the $2,500 is less than the $3,200 those missed calls cost you every month." |
| **"My clients book on WhatsApp, not the phone."** | "We cover WhatsApp too — auto-reply, book, confirm. Same assistant, both channels. The phone calls still leak; plugging both is the point." |
| **"I've tried an answering service before — clients hated it."** | "Most services use a script reader in a call centre. This speaks in your voice, knows your price list, and books appointments — not a generic 'please hold'." |
| **"It's too expensive for Dar es Salaam."** | "It's priced for shops doing $50K+/year in services — which you are if your average ticket is TZS 150K and you're busy. One premium-treatment slot covers the monthly fee." |
| **"Can I try it before committing?"** | "Yes. Free 3-day call audit on your existing line, then a 14-day trial. You see the numbers before you pay anything." |
| **"I need to talk to my partner/manager first."** | "Makes sense. I'll send you a one-page summary with the numbers. When's a good time to follow up with both of you on the line?" |
