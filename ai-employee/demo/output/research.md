## Topic: AI Meeting-Note Tools 2026 — best pick for the team

*Why it matters: cutting the team's admin time on notes/actions from ~20 calls a week.*

### Overview
The 2026 market splits into two camps: **transcription + search platforms** (Fireflies, Otter) and **video/clip-first recorders** (tl;dv, Grain). A third, newer group is "bot-free" local tools (Fathom, Granola) that record without a meeting bot joining.

### Why It Matters to Us
We run ~20 internal + client meetings/week. A notetaker that auto-captures, extracts actions, and syncs to our workflow would reclaim an estimated 3–5 hours/week of admin across the team — at $8–20/seat/month.

### 3 Things Worth Knowing
1. **Fireflies.ai** is the strongest all-rounder for teams: 91–97% transcription accuracy, 100+ languages, deep CRM/integration layer, $10/seat/mo — best if we want searchable history and automation ([1](https://tldv.io/blog/fireflies-vs-otter/), [2](https://www.umevo.ai/blogs/ume-all-posts/otter-vs-notta-vs-fireflies-vs-tl-dv-the-ultimate-2026-comparison-for-meeting-transcription)).
2. **tl;dv** has the best free tier (unlimited recordings, 30+ languages, video clips) at $18/seat/mo — best if we want to pilot for free first ([2](https://www.umevo.ai/blogs/ume-all-posts/otter-vs-notta-vs-fireflies-vs-tl-dv-the-ultimate-2026-comparison-for-meeting-transcription), [4](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/fireflies-vs-tldv/)).
3. **Otter.ai** is cheaper and simple ($8.33/user/mo, 300 free min/mo) but weaker for teams: only 3–6 languages, no custom templates, weaker automation ([1](https://tldv.io/blog/fireflies-vs-otter/), [3](https://trendharvest.blog/blog/best-ai-meeting-assistants-otter-vs-fireflies-vs-tldv-2026)).

### Recommendation (for your review)
Pilot **tl;dv** (free) for 2 weeks with 3 team members. If adoption sticks, move to **Fireflies** ($10/seat/mo) for team-wide search + CRM sync. Budget impact: ~$40–60/mo for a 4–6 seat pilot.
