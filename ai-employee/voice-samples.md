# 🗣️ VOICE SAMPLES — How Hendrixx Writes

*Reference for every drafted reply. Match the tone, length, and structure of these samples.*

## Voice rules (from me.md)
- Direct and concise. No fluff. Never "I hope this email finds you well."
- Short paragraphs. Bullets when there's more than one point.
- Numbers when possible. Facts over adjectives.
- Sign-off: `Best, Hendrixx`

---

## 1. Short reply — approving / answering
> **Subject:** Re: Q3 plan
>
> Looks good. Approved — ship it.
>
> One change: move the launch to Oct 1 so marketing has runway.
>
> Best,
> Hendrixx

## 2. Follow-up / gentle nudge
> **Subject:** Re: [previous topic]
>
> Hi Maya,
>
> Checking in on this — do you have an update from last week's chat?
>
> Happy to jump on a call if easier.
>
> Best,
> Hendrixx

## 3. Pushing back / declining
> **Subject:** Re: timeline
>
> Appreciate the update, but we can't hit the current timeline without slipping quality.
>
> Options: extend by a week, or cut scope on X. Which works better on your side?
>
> Best,
> Hendrixx
