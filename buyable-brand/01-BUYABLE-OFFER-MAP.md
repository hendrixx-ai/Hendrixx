# The Buyable Offer Map
## Lead-magnet copy deck (paste into Canva → export PDF)

**File name to publish:** `The-Buyable-Offer-Map.pdf`  
**Format:** 1 worksheet page + optional 2nd “example + next step” page  
**Size:** 1080×1920 (phone) **and** US Letter / A4 (email). Design the Letter version first; crop a vertical IG version from the same copy.  
**Voice:** direct, no guru. One offer. One beginner. One sentence.

---

# PAGE 1 — The Map (this is the product)

## Eyebrow
THE BUYABLE BRAND · FREE MAP

## Headline
Fill this in and you will have  
**one sentence you could sell this month.**

## Subhead
Not a vibe. Not a bio. An offer.  
If a stranger cannot buy the sentence, it is not finished.

## Intro (3 lines, small type)
Most beginners build a pretty account with nothing at checkout.  
This page flips the order: **offer first, aesthetic second, audience third.**  
Write in ink. First draft is supposed to be ugly.

---

## 1. WHO
**Label:** One beginner. Not “everyone.” Not “creatives.”  
**Prompt:** I help ________________________________.  
**Helper text:** Age-ish + situation + skill they already have.  
**Example (tiny, italic):** *ambitious beginners with taste (design, photo, video, writing, style) and no product*

**Checkbox filter (optional micro-row):**
- [ ] I can find 100 of them on Instagram this week
- [ ] They already feel behind
- [ ] They have a card they will spend on a first win

---

## 2. RESULT
**Label:** What they can point at in 14 days or less.  
**Prompt:** so they can ________________________________.  
**Helper text:** Visible outcome. Not “feel confident.” Not “level up.”  
**Examples:** *name and upload their first digital product · open a checkout link · rewrite a bio that can take payments*

---

## 3. WITHOUT
**Label:** The fear that keeps them lurking.  
**Prompt:** without ________________________________.  
**Helper text:** The thing they think they must do first.  
**Examples:** *10k followers · looking like a slimy guru · buying Kajabi · becoming an influencer · a 40-hour course*

---

## 4. FORMAT
**Label:** What they actually hand over. Circle one.

| ○ Template pack | ○ Swipe file / scripts | ○ Mini-course (under 90 min) |
| ○ Notion system | ○ Weekend kit (hybrid) | ○ Audit / teardown PDF |

**Helper text:** If you are starting from zero, circle **Weekend kit** or **Templates**. Do not circle “community.”

---

## 5. PRICE
**Label:** What a stranger would pay before they trust you.

| ○ $27 first product | ○ $47 template system | ○ $97 with video |
| ○ $147 full install | ○ Other: $______ |

**Helper text:** If you have 0 sales, you do not get to start at $497. Pride is not a pricing strategy.

---

## 6. THE SENTENCE
**Label:** Copy this. This is the offer.

> I help **[WHO]**  
> get **[RESULT]**  
> without **[WITHOUT]**.

**Write yours here:**

> I help ______________________________________  
> get ______________________________________  
> without ______________________________________.

**Smell test (they must tick all three):**
- [ ] A stranger knows if it’s for them in 3 seconds
- [ ] They can imagine the file they receive
- [ ] I would not be embarrassed to say this on a Reel tomorrow

---

## How to use the map in 20 minutes
1. Fill boxes 1–5 without editing. Ugly is correct.  
2. Write the sentence once. Read it out loud. Cut one adjective.  
3. Write the bio line. Put it on Instagram today. Not “when the grid matches.”  
4. Write 5 hooks. Film one tomorrow.  
5. Reply to the email (or the DM) with your sentence. I will tell you if it is buyable.

## What this map is not
A personality test. A 40-page workbook. A promise you will make $10k.  
It is a **sentence + a price + five hooks**. That is enough to start selling.
