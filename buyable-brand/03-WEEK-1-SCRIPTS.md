# Week 1 — word-for-word scripts
## The Buyable Brand · Days 1–7

**How to film:** phone, vertical, window on your face, captions on. One location. One outfit is fine for the whole week.  
**Length:** 25–45 seconds unless noted. Cut any breath that does not earn the next line.  
**On-screen text:** first line of each script is the hook. Keep it up for 3 seconds.  
**Audio:** talk to one ambitious beginner, not “you guys.”  
**After every post:** reply to every comment for 30 minutes like a human.

Replace `[HANDLE]`, `[MAP LINK]`, `[STORE LINK]`.

# Sunday batch card (do this the day before Day 1)

**90 minutes**

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 0–15 | Write nothing new. These scripts are done. Highlight any line that doesn’t sound like your mouth and swap one word. |
| 15–40 | Film Days 1, 3, 4, 6, 7 in one take each. Clap between takes. |
| 40–65 | CapCut: caption all 5. Export. |
| 65–85 | Build Day 2 + Day 5 carousels in Canva (copy is above). |
| 85–90 | Schedule or dump in a folder named `WEEK-1`. Write the 4 Story frames for Day 1 only. |

**On-camera notes**
- Start talking before you hit record, then cut the lead-in.  
- If you smile after a hard line, keep it. If you apologize, delete that take.  
- Do not say “Hey guys.” Do not say “in this economy.” Do not say “I’m so excited to share.”
