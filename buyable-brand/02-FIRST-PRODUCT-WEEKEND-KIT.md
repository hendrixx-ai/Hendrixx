# Video breakdown (record as looms / phone talking-head)

Keep each video under 15 minutes. Script the first 10 seconds. No intro music.

### Video 1 — The rule (6–8 min)
**Title:** Ugly v1 or you stay unpaid  
**Teach:**
- Offer first, aesthetic second, audience third
- What “done” means: a named file + a link that takes money
- Tools: Docs or Notion, Canva, CapCut, Stan Store or Gumroad, Instagram
- How to use the Weekend Clock (do not skip around)
**On-screen:** the “done” definition  
**CTA inside video:** pause, create the folder, come back

### Video 2 — Pick the thing (12–14 min)
**Title:** One idea. Kill the rest.  
**Teach:**
- Three sources: what people already ask / what you had to figure out the hard way / what you can template
- The 3-filter test: you can ship it this weekend / a beginner will pay $27 / you can talk about it for 30 days
- Idea graveyard (why “everything I know about success” fails)
- Walk through the Product Picker live with **your** example
**Output:** one sentence offer  
**Worksheet:** Product Picker (below)

### Video 3 — Build v1 (14–16 min)
**Title:** What’s in the zip (and what you will cut)  
**Teach:**
- Minimum viable digital product: 1 promise, 1 walkthrough, 1 template, 1 next step
- Section-by-section using the Outline Template
- How long it should take to consume (under 90 minutes or it is a course — cut it)
- Recording tips: phone, window, one take per section, captions later if ever
**Output:** filled outline + first draft started

### Video 4 — Make it look buyable, not cinematic (8–10 min)
**Title:** A cover that can sit next to a $27 price  
**Teach:**
- Name on the cover. Promise under it. No stock sunset.
- How to use the Canva pack (duplicate, swap colors, export PDF + PNG)
- Mockup = they can see the thing. That’s the whole job.
- Thank-you page = “start here” + upsell seed (The Buyable Brand, soft)
**Output:** cover PNG + mockup PNG

### Video 5 — Open the store (10–12 min)
**Title:** Checkout before you feel ready  
**Teach:**
- Stan vs Gumroad in 60 seconds (pick one, do not research)
- Paste the sales-page swipe, add 3 bullets from their sentence
- Price: $27 unless they have a reason
- Delivery folder permissions (anyone with link / purchaser only)
- Test purchase on your phone
**Output:** live URL

### Video 6 — Tell the internet (10–12 min)
**Title:** First sales with a small account  
**Teach:**
- Who to DM: friends, old coworkers, people who already ask you questions, 10 commenters on niche posts
- How to post without a launch sequence from hell
- Use the 5 Reel scripts + 12 Stories
- What to say when someone says “nice” and does not buy
**Soft upsell (last 90 seconds):**  
“If you want the profile, the content pillars, and the Reel → DM → checkout system installed around this product, that’s The Buyable Brand. $147. Link is in the thank-you page. Finish this weekend first.”
