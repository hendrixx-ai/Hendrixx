## The Problem (in the customer's words)

> *"I receive ten gold offers a week from Africa. Nine are scams or brokers. Before I reply to anyone, I ask: can this supplier's paperwork survive my compliance officer, my refinery's audit, and my own lab?"*

That's the reality for every international gold buyer (Research §Pain Points 1–3): advance-fee traps, forged assays, below-market bait, smuggling stigma, mandatory DMCC/OECD due diligence on their side. **Verification is not a step in their buying process — it IS the buying process.** Most supplier marketing fails it in the first sentence.

## The Core Promise

> **Tanzanian gold supply built to pass verification — independent assay, buyer-chosen testing, complete export documentation, and index-honest pricing from the Kahama gold belt.**

One sentence, honest, benefit-led. Every word is deliverable today or a documented process step (see Honesty Check). What we promise is *verifiability* — never investment returns, never "deals."

## Risk Reversal / Guarantee (DRAFT — confirm you can honor every line)

Classic "30-day money-back" doesn't fit a quote-based commodity trade. Our risk reversal is **structural — we remove the buyer's three biggest risks before money moves:**

> **The Verification-First Terms (draft):**
> 1. **No speculative advance fees.** We never ask a buyer to pay "taxes," "clearance," or "UN certificates" before shipment — the #1 scam pattern (Research §Pain Point 1). Regulatory costs are handled in the documented, receipted export process, not extracted from buyers in advance.
> 2. **Test before you commit.** Independent assay + samples before contract signature; buyers may use their own lab.
> 3. **Verify us before you trust us.** Licence registry checks, KYC, references — welcomed, not resisted.
> 4. **Payment against documents** via buyer-preferred secure structures (LC/escrow options to be confirmed case-by-case — **Hendrixx must confirm which instruments he accepts before this line goes public**).

⚠️ *Lines 1–3 are process commitments Hendrixx must confirm he operates by. Line 4 names instruments not yet confirmed — do not publish until confirmed.*

## Price Framing (honest, quote-based)

- **We quote against the index.** Prices derive from the LBMA fix (or agreed benchmark) at time of contract — the documented norm for UAE refinery consignments (Research Sources: Zaara). No invented "discounts."
- **We never claim below-market prices.** Below-market is the #1 documented scam tell (Research §Pain Point 4) — our honest pricing is itself a trust signal and a competitive separator.
- **Costs are transparent, not hidden:** export royalties and fees are real, receipted costs of legal export (rates TO CONFIRM at madini.go.tz — sources conflict: 6%+1% vs 4% vs differentiated 2%/4%; Research ⚠️ Assumption 2). Until confirmed, no rate appears in public copy.
- **No fake anchors, no urgency.** Quotes carry a validity window (standard practice), never pressure.
