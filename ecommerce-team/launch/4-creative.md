**Creative principle for this category:** in a market where buyers are trained to detect scams, *the ad itself must not look like a scam ad*. No gold-bar glamour shots, no "discount" energy, no urgency. Calm, typographic, document-led creative is the differentiator (Research §Competitors — the field's red-flag marketing makes restraint stand out).

---

## Creative Angles (tied to research)

1. **Documentation-first** (Angle 1) — the document package is the hero
2. **Anti-scam education** (Angles 1+4) — teach the red flags, position us as the answer
3. **"Verify us — we insist"** (Angle 2) — the invitation to hostile scrutiny
4. **Kahama origin** (Angle 3) — the real gold belt, map-led
5. **The compliance-officer test** (Angle 5) — speak to the person who actually blocks deals

## Concept 1 — "The Five Documents" ⭐ (lead concept)

**One-line idea:** The export document package, itemized like a checklist, as the ad.

**Image direction (1:1, 4:5):** Clean off-white background. Five document cards stacked slightly fanned, each labeled: *Export Permit · Certificate of Origin · Assay Report · Royalty Receipts · Chain of Custody*. Type: dark charcoal, one accent color (deep green). Logo + "Kahama, Tanzania" small at base. No gold imagery.

**Video (20s storyboard):**
- 0–2s **HOOK:** white frame, black type: *"A gold offer is only as good as its paperwork."*
- 3–8s — each of the 5 documents slides in on beat, labeled
- 9–14s — *"If a supplier can't show you these, walk away."*
- 15–20s — *"Tanzanian gold supply built to pass verification. We send our documents first."* + CTA: **Request a Quote**

**Hook variations (overlay/first-line):**
1. "If a gold offer can't show you these five documents, walk away."
2. "The paperwork serious gold buyers check first."
3. "Five documents. One verified supply."

**Assets needed:** typographic only — executable in Canva today. No shooting required.

## Concept 2 — "Below Market = Walk Away"

**One-line idea:** Anti-scam education — name the #1 scam tell, own the opposite.

**Image direction (1:1, 9:16):** Red-flag listicle look: *"5 SIGNS A GOLD OFFER IS A SCAM"* — ① below-market price ② advance fees ③ unverifiable licence ④ broker chains ⑤ pressure tactics. Items ①–⑤ in red strike-through style; footer in green: *"We do the opposite of all five."*

**Video (25s storyboard):**
- 0–2s **HOOK:** *"How to spot a fake gold offer in 10 seconds."*
- 3–15s — the five red flags, one per beat, red X marks
- 16–21s — *"We quote off the LBMA benchmark. No advance fees. One principal you can verify."*
- 22–25s — CTA: **See how we quote → Request a Quote**

**Hook variations:**
1. "The #1 sign a gold 'deal' is a scam."
2. "If the price is below market, the gold doesn't exist."
3. "What 10 seconds of due diligence saves you."

**Assets needed:** typographic only — executable today.

## Concept 4 — "From Kahama"

**One-line idea:** Origin credibility — a map-led route from Tanzania's gold belt to the buyer's port.

**Image direction (4:5, 1:1):** Minimal map graphic: **KAHAMA → DAR ES SALAAM → YOUR PORT.** Route line in accent green. Sub-line: *"One of Tanzania's highest-volume gold trading regions."* Footer: documented supply, mine-to-export traceability.

**Video (15s storyboard):**
- 0–2s **HOOK:** *"Where does your gold actually come from?"*
- 3–9s — map animates: Kahama (gold belt) → Dar es Salaam → marker at "your port"
- 10–15s — *"One principal. One documented chain. From Kahama to you."* + CTA

**Hook variations:**
1. "Where does your gold actually come from?"
2. "From Tanzania's gold belt to your refinery."
3. "One accountable principal. From Kahama."

**Assets needed:** map graphic (Canva/Figma) — executable today. Optional upgrade: 5–10s real footage of the region/road (needs shooting).

## Concept 5 — "The Compliance Officer Test"

**One-line idea:** Speak directly to the person who actually blocks deals — the compliance/AML officer.

**Image direction (1:1, LinkedIn-first):** Mimic an audit checklist (monospace type, minimal): *"Permit ✓ Origin ✓ Assay ✓ Receipts ✓ Chain of custody ✓"* — header: *"Your compliance officer will ask for five things. We lead with them."*

**Video (20s storyboard):**
- 0–2s **HOOK:** *"Your compliance officer is about to reject this ad."*
- 3–14s — checklist items tick off one by one as narrator names them
- 15–20s — *"Make their job easy. That's our whole strategy."* + CTA: **Request a Quote**

**Hook variations:**
1. "Your compliance officer will ask for five things. We lead with them."
2. "The gold offer your AML team won't flag."
3. "Make onboarding easy. Start with documents."
