## Objections / FAQ

**1. How is the price set?**
Against the LBMA benchmark (or an agreed index) at time of contract. Quotes carry a standard validity window — no pressure, no "discounts." You'll never see a below-market price from us; that's a scam signal, not a deal.

**2. What documents come with a shipment?**
The package international buyers and UAE import rules require: export permit, certificate of origin, independent assay report, royalty/fee receipts, and chain-of-custody records. Exact regulatory costs are confirmed and receipted at contract stage.

**3. Can we use our own assayer or lab?**
Yes — that's the preferred way. Assay certificates and samples are available on request, and witnessed or buyer-chosen testing arrangements can be discussed during the RFQ.

**4. Do you require advance fees before shipment?**
No. We do not request speculative "tax," "clearance," or "certificate" payments from buyers — regulatory costs are handled inside the documented export process, with receipts.

**5. What volumes, grades, and forms do you supply?**
Each RFQ states form, grade, and volume, and we confirm honestly what we can meet before anything else happens. If we can't supply your spec, we say so.

**6. Which destinations do you serve?**
International export — typically UAE, and other destinations as agreed. State your destination and port in the RFQ.

**7. How do we know you're real?**
Exactly the right question. Before any commitment: we share our document package, we welcome your KYC process, and we invite registry verification through Tanzania's mining authorities. Deals move at the speed of your verification — not our sales pitch.

**8. Can we visit or inspect?**
Inspection arrangements for qualified buyers can be discussed at RFQ stage — verification in person is never discouraged.
