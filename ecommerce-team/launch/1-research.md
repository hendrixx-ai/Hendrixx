## The Ideal Customer

**Primary: international gold buyers — UAE-based refiners/trading houses first, then Switzerland, India.** This is where the physical flows already go: SwissAid data shows the UAE alone received **405 t of the 435 t of gold smuggled out of Africa in 2022** — about **47% of all African gold exports to non-African countries** go to the UAE ([SwissAid via Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/uae-receives-most-africas-smuggled-gold-says-report), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/05/30/billions-of-dollars-of-african-gold-smuggled-into-uae-yearly-research.html)). Secondary customer per `product.md`: investors + mining-company owners.

**Who they are in practice:**
- **Refiners & trading houses (UAE/Switzerland/India)** — buy doré/bullion continuously; legally required to run due diligence on suppliers (see Pain Point 1)
- **Licensed precious-metals traders (DMCC members, DGD-accredited refiners)** — must document mine-to-export chain of custody
- **Investors seeking direct supply** — highest scam-anxiety of the three (see Pain Point 2)

**What they are NOT:** retail consumers. This rules out consumer-store tactics entirely — the "product page" is a supply/RFQ landing page.

## Pain Points (ranked)

**1. Verification risk — "is this supplier real, licensed, and legal?" (THE blocker)**
The African gold trade is saturated with fraud. Documented scam patterns include advance-fee fraud, forged assay certificates, forged export licences, sample substitution, fictitious mines, and below-market-price bait ([African Gold Advisory — 11 scam patterns](https://www.africangoldadvisory.com/blog/african-gold-scams-how-they-work), [ZenGold fraud log](https://zengold.com/gold-fraud/)). Legitimate buyers are *trained* to treat unsolicited African gold offers as guilty until proven innocent. **Any supplier that cannot survive hostile verification does not get a reply.**

**2. Regulatory exposure on the buyer's side.**
UAE refineries face mandatory responsible-sourcing audits (DMCC Practical Guidance implementing the [OECD 5-step due diligence framework](https://www.oecd.org/daf/inv/mne/Gold-Industry-and-Sector-Initiatives-Guide.pdf)); UAE now requires yearly audits of all gold refineries and KYC/AML registration (goAML) for precious-metals dealers ([AML UAE](https://amluae.com/to-curb-illegitimate-trade-uae-to-audit-all-gold-refineries/)). A Tanzanian supplier that arrives with incomplete paperwork is *work* for the buyer's compliance officer; a supplier with a complete, OECD-aligned document package is *easy to onboard*. Buyers reject suppliers who create compliance risk.

**3. Smuggling stigma.**
With $24–35 B/year of African ASM gold going undeclared — 80–85% of it flowing to the UAE ([SwissAid via CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/05/30/billions-of-dollars-of-african-gold-smuggled-into-uae-yearly-research.html)) — every African-origin offer starts under suspicion. The buyer's question underneath every deal: *"can this gold's paperwork survive an audit?"*

**4. Price-bait fatigue.**
"Below world market prices" is the #1 listed scam tell ([African Gold Advisory](https://www.africangoldadvisory.com/blog/african-gold-scams-how-they-work)) — yet it's the headline most Tanzanian seller sites lead with (see Competitors). Serious buyers filter out below-market offers *automatically*. Honest, index-linked pricing is actually a differentiator.

**5. Broker fog.**
Layered intermediary chains obscure who actually owns the gold ([African Gold Advisory](https://www.africangoldadvisory.com/blog/african-gold-scams-how-they-work)). Buyers want to reach a principal who can produce documents, not a daisy-chain of agents.

The field either oversells (below-market bait) or undersells (institutions with no outbound marketing). A Kahama-based supplier who leads with *verifiable licences, independent assay, transparent royalty/permit receipts, buyer-chosen verification, and OECD/DMCC-ready paperwork* occupies empty, high-trust territory. The competitor set's own red-flag marketing makes honest positioning stand out more.

## Buying Triggers (what makes them buy / what stops them)

**Triggers:**
1. **Verifiable licensing** — dealer licence checkable with the Mining Commission / Ministry of Minerals ([verification routes](https://goldbuyersafrica.com/gold-sellers-in-tanzania/))
2. **Independent assay** — TMAA/government-approved or SGS-style certificates ([procedures](https://goldbarsuppliers.com/gold-export-procedures-in-africa/)); buyers increasingly insist on *their own* lab or witnessed testing ([African Gold Advisory](https://www.africangoldadvisory.com/blog/african-gold-scams-how-thery-work))
3. **Complete export document package** — export permit, certificate of origin, assay report, royalty/tax receipts, AML/KYC records, chain of custody — exactly what UAE/Dubai import requires ([Dubai documentation checklist](https://www.buygoldbarsafrica.com/dubai-gold-export-rules/), [Zaara Consulting](https://zaara-consulting.com/import-gold-from-africa-to-uae/))
4. **Transparent, index-linked pricing** (LBMA-fix-based valuation is the documented norm for UAE refinery consignments — [Zaara](https://zaara-consulting.com/import-gold-from-africa-to-uae/)) rather than "discounts"
5. **Welcome KYC/AML** — counterintuitive but real: a supplier who *asks for* the buyer's due diligence signals legitimacy ([DMCC/OECD framework](https://www.oecd.org/daf/inv/mne/Gold-Industry-and-Sector-Initiatives-Guide.pdf))
6. **Invitation to inspect** — physical presence and sample inspection in a regulated office (the INPIN model)

**Stoppers:** advance-fee requests, below-market pricing, unverifiable licence claims, pressure tactics, refusal of third-party testing, broker chains.

## Angles Worth Testing (ranked, evidence-tied)

1. **"Documentation-first."** Lead every touchpoint with the document package: licence → assay → permit → receipts → chain of custody. Directly answers Pain Points 1–3; matches UAE onboarding reality. *(Strongest — evidence: buyer-side due diligence mandates.)*
2. **"Verify us — we insist."** Buyer-chosen lab, Mining Commission license check invitation, KYC welcomed. Converts the scam landscape into the pitch: *the safest offer you'll receive this month is one that invites hostile scrutiny.* (Evidence: scam-pattern literacy among buyers.)
3. **"Kahama gold belt, mine-to-export traceability."** Located in one of Tanzania's biggest ASM gold trading hubs (IPIS mapping lists Kahama among the highest-volume trading towns; elution plants operate there) ([IPIS](https://ipisresearch.be/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/1901-ASM-Tanzania_web%C2%AE.pdf)); Tanzania is Africa's 4th-largest producer — 61.6 t in 2024, ~45 M oz reserves ([TanzaniaInvest](https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/gold/page/7)). Proximity = shorter, auditable chain.
4. **"Priced honestly off the index — no below-market fairy tales."** Index/LBMA-fix-based quoting only. Anti-scam-bait positioning; instantly separates from tier-2 competitors. (Evidence: below-market = documented critical scam pattern.)
5. **"OECD/DMCC-ready supply."** Package and present material to match downstream refiners' responsible-sourcing audit needs — become the easy supplier to onboard. (Evidence: mandatory refinery audits in UAE.)
