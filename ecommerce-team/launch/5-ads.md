## 1. Platform Recommendation

**Start: LinkedIn Ads — primary. Why:** the customer is a B2B decision-maker (refiner/trading-house procurement, compliance, precious-metals traders) reachable by job title and industry — LinkedIn is where that targeting actually exists, and the "Compliance Officer Test" creative is LinkedIn-native (Research §Ideal Customer). UAE/Switzerland/India + international English-speaking B2B.

**Second: Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — secondary, investor segment.** Broader, cheaper reach; use only the education-style concepts (1, 2, 4). Riskier audience quality for a high-trust category — hence second, with strict qualification in the RFQ form.

**Skip for now:** TikTok (wrong register for B2B commodity buyers), Google Search ads (defer until the landing page is live and indexed — search volume for "gold supplier Tanzania" is dominated by the tier-2 sellers; winning that auction game is a later project).

**Zero-budget complement (do this regardless):** LinkedIn organic posts using the same creative + the existing `../ai-employee/` outreach system — the CRM sequences and research pipeline already built for exactly this audience. Paid ads amplify a working organic + outreach motion; they don't replace it.

## 2. Campaign Structure

**LinkedIn (one campaign to start):**
- Campaign: `HG-SUPPLY | RFQ | Intl | {date}` — objective: **Lead Generation** (native RFQ form = lower friction than sending B2B buyers to a landing page) or Website Conversions once the page is live — pick ONE
- Ad group 1: `DOC-01` — Concepts 1+5 (documents/compliance)
- Ad group 2: `EDU-01` — Concept 2 (anti-scam education)
- Placements: LinkedIn Feed only (Audience Network off — placement control in a trust-sensitive category)
- Language: English · Geo: UAE first, then Switzerland, India, UK — one geo per ad group so results stay readable

**Meta (phase 2, only after LinkedIn learnings):**
- Campaign: `HG-SUPPLY-META | RFQ | Intl | {date}` — objective: Leads (instant form)
- One ad set, education concepts only (1, 2, 4)

**Naming rule:** every ad name carries concept + hook number (e.g. `C1-H2`) so results map back to creative without guessing.

**Exclusions:** company employees, existing contacts, job-seeker categories (Meta).

## 5. Creative-Test Plan (one variable at a time)

- **Test 1 (weeks 1–2): message angle.** DOC-01 vs EDU-01, same audience, same geo. Question: do document-led or education-led hooks earn more qualified RFQ starts?
- **Test 2 (weeks 3–4): hooks within the winner.** Same concept, V1 vs V2 vs V3. Question: which hook framing pulls?
- **Test 3 (only if volume supports): geo.** Winner vs second geo (CH or IN).
- **Judge nothing before each test window closes** — B2B volume is small; early kills are noise kills.
- Concept 3 ("Verify Us", on-camera) joins when the footage is shot — expect it to be the strongest trust asset once live; don't wait for it to start testing the typographic set.

## 6. Starting Budget Framework (you decide the number — none is invented for you)

Budget reality is **TO CONFIRM** in product.md, so here is the decision framework:

| Tier | What it's for | How to allocate | Your go/no-go rule (set before spending) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Minimum viable** | Test 1 only | 100% LinkedIn DOC-01 vs EDU-01 | "If no *qualified* RFQ start after $___ spend, pause and reassess" |
| **Standard** | Tests 1–2 | ~70% LinkedIn / 30% reserve for winning hook | "If a hook produces a qualified RFQ at ≤ $___ per start, scale that ad set" |
| **Growth** | + Meta phase 2 | Cap Meta at ⅓ of total until its leads prove as qualified as LinkedIn's | "Meta pauses if 2 weeks pass with zero qualified forms" |

**Set the $ blanks BEFORE launch and write them down.** Platform minimum daily budgets apply — check the current minimums inside LinkedIn Campaign Manager / Meta Ads Manager when setting up (don't rely on third-party figures). Expect small B2B volume: this category trades a handful of serious RFQs, not thousands of clicks.

## 7. Measurement — what "working" means, decided BEFORE spending

**Track from day one:**
1. **Qualified RFQ starts** — define NOW: *form submitted with a stated spec (form/volume) + company + destination.* Anything less = unqualified, no matter how cheap
2. Cost per qualified RFQ start (spend ÷ #1)
3. RFQs converting to real conversations (email/LinkedIn reply from a named person)
4. CTR & form-start rate by concept/hook (diagnostic only — never the goal)
5. Emails captured (for the Email Agent's sequences — the real asset)

**"Working" = qualified RFQ starts at or under the cost-per-lead you set in §6, that turn into conversations.** Clicks, likes, and cheap unqualified leads are explicitly NOT success. **No ROAS exists yet** — first shipments are months out; revenue attribution comes later, from real data only.
