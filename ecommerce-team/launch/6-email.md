## Welcome Sequence (3 emails — for new contacts from the landing page / ads / opt-in)

### W1 — "The five documents" (day 0, immediately on opt-in)

**Subject:** The five documents serious gold buyers check first

Thanks for connecting. Whether you're a refiner, trader, or investor — before you buy gold from anyone (including me), ask for five documents:

1. Export permit
2. Certificate of origin
3. Independent assay report
4. Royalty/fee receipts
5. Chain-of-custody records

If a supplier can't show them, walk away. I built my supply offer around the opposite principle: we share our documents first and invite your verification — your lab, your KYC, your registry checks.

[Request a Quote — send your spec]

Best, Hendrixx
Hendrixx · Kahama, Tanzania

**Subject:** Why I'll never quote you a "below market" price

If that's how you want to buy, send me your spec — form, volume, destination — and I'll tell you honestly what I can supply.

[Send your RFQ]

Best, Hendrixx

## RFQ Follow-Up Sequence (3 emails — contact started an RFQ / asked for a quote, then went quiet)

### F1 — the nudge (day 2 after silence)

**Subject:** Your RFQ — anything blocking it?
**Preview:** One question, honestly answered.

Hi [Name],

You started a quote request with us. If something's holding you back — verification questions, spec doubts, timing — tell me what it is and I'll answer it straight.

If the deal's simply not right right now, that's fine too. A clear no always beats silence.

Best, Hendrixx

**CTA:** reply to this email · **Timing:** 2 days after silence

### F2 — objection handling (day 6)

**Subject:** The three things buyers usually ask first
**Preview:** Answers before you have to ask.

Hi [Name],

Whenever an RFQ pauses, it's usually one of three questions:

**"Are you real?"** — Fair. Before any commitment, we share our document package, welcome your KYC process, and invite verification through Tanzania's mining authorities. Deal speed is set by your due diligence, not my pitch.
**"Do you want advance fees?"** — No. Never. Regulatory costs are receipted steps inside the documented export process.
**"How is the price set?"** — Off the LBMA benchmark at contract time. No below-market bait.

If question four is the blocker, I'd genuinely like to hear it.

Best, Hendrixx

**CTA:** reply · **Timing:** 6 days after silence

### F3 — the close-the-loop (day 12, final)

**Subject:** Closing your file (and one honest summary)
**Preview:** No hard feelings — one page you can keep.

Hi [Name],

I'll close this RFQ for now — no follow-up chase from my side.

One thing to keep: the five-document checklist (export permit, certificate of origin, assay report, royalty receipts, chain of custody). It protects you with any supplier, anywhere, forever.

If a future shipment need matches what I supply from Kahama, my door's open.

Best, Hendrixx

**CTA:** none (graceful exit) · **Timing:** 12 days after silence

## Post-Contract Sequence (3 emails — after a signed deal; timing per deal stage)

### P1 — confirmation & what happens next (on signature)

**Subject:** Confirmed — and here's exactly what happens next
**Preview:** Your document timeline, in writing.

Hi [Name],

Confirmed. Here's the path from here, in writing:

1. Assay per the agreed process (your lab welcome) — [date window per contract]
2. Export documentation assembled: permit, origin certificate, receipts — [per contract]
3. Shipment/logistics per agreed terms — [per contract]

Every step gets a document. Every document gets sent to you when it exists — not before, not after.

Best, Hendrixx

**CTA:** n/a · **Timing:** day of signature

### P2 — the honest review ask (only after successful delivery — no early asks)

**Subject:** How did we do — honestly?
**Preview:** One short question.

Hi [Name],

Now that [shipment] has landed and been processed on your side: how did the documentation and process hold up to your verification?

A short, honest answer helps me improve — and if the process earned it, a reference or referral means more than any advertisement.

Best, Hendrixx

**CTA:** reply · **Timing:** only after confirmed delivery & processing — never before

## Compliance Reminders (apply to EVERY email above — non-negotiable)

1. **Unsubscribe / opt-out link** in the footer of every send — one click, honored promptly
2. **Physical business address** in the footer (standard anti-spam requirement)
3. **Opt-in only** — email only people who signed up, submitted an RFQ, or have an existing business relationship; buying lead lists is a compliance and reputation risk in this category
4. **No unsolicited bulk "cold blasts"** through this sequence — for cold outreach, use one-to-one emails via the existing `../ai-employee/` CRM (built for that), not this list
5. Keep records of consent — the email tool does this if the sequence is set up correctly
6. Test-send every sequence to yourself before activating
