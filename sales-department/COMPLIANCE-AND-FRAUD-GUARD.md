# 🛡️ GOLD TRADE — Compliance & Fraud Guard

*Read this before engaging any buyer. Gold is a high-fraud, high-regulation trade —
being "faster, not spammier" also means **never getting scammed or non-compliant.**
This is operational guidance, not legal advice — verify current rules with your export
agent / a licensed mineral lawyer before relying on specifics.*

---

## 1 · Licensing (Tanzania) — know which one YOU hold

| Licence | What it allows | Cannot |
|---------|----------------|--------|
| **Broker** | Buy from authorised miners at buying stations; sell to licensed dealers | ❌ Cannot export |
| **Dealer** | Buy from miners/brokers; **can export** | — |
| **PML** (small miner) | Mine + sell at Mineral & Gem House auction centres | — |

→ **Know which licence you operate under and never act outside it.** If you're
supplying doré across borders, you (or your counterparty) must hold a dealer/export
licence. Exporting on a broker licence is illegal.

---

## 2 · KYC / AML on every buyer (before first deal)

- Verify the buyer is a real, licensed entity (check company registry, refinery
  accreditation, references). Don't take a website at face value.
- Confirm the actual buyer of record — not an intermediary claiming to "represent" a
  refinery.
- For Dubai/UAE: confirm DMCC / Dubai Good Delivery status via official lists, not a
  screenshot they send you.
- Keep a record: who you dealt with, their licence/registration, and the trail of
  communication.

---

## 3 · Fraud red flags 🚩 — walk away if you see these

- **Advance-fee scam:** "pay a fee/refundable deposit to unlock the deal / register / get
  the export permit." Legit buyers don't ask you to pay to sell to them.
- **Fake LOI / PO / ICPO:** impressive-looking but unverifiable letters of intent from
  "buyers" — often a setup for a fee request.
- **"Official" who needs a bribe / facilitation fee** to release funds or permits.
- **Unrealistic offers:** price far above spot, or "guaranteed" returns.
- **Pressure tactics:** "act today or the buyer walks."
- **Sloppy or mismatched emails** — refinery names at gmail.com, misspelled domains,
  "double your money" language. (Note: Emirates Gold's own site now warns about
  impersonation domains — a live example of why this matters.)
- **Requests to ship before payment** clears, or vague payment promises.

---

## 4 · Payment security — never release goods without confirmed funds

- Prefer **irrevocable LC (letter of credit)**, escrow, or **partial deposit + balance on
  BL copy** for first deals.
- Never ship on a promise, a "proof of funds" screenshot, or a forwarded bank message.
- Confirm the receiving bank and beneficiary match the buyer of record.
- Start small: a **trial lot** de-risks both sides.

---

## 5 · Export documentation & responsible sourcing

- Keep a complete package per lot: **export permit, assay certificate, origin
  documentation, commercial invoice, packing list.**
- **Responsible sourcing:** be able to show your gold's origin and that it isn't tied to
  conflict or illegal artisanal mining. ICGLR/OECD due-diligence documentation is what
  serious refiners (and UAE regulators) ask for.
- **VAT note:** domestic gold supply to local refineries/smelters is zero-rated (Finance
  Act 2024) — but confirm your specific transaction's treatment.

---

## ❌ Never do this (the hard list)

- Never pay to sell. Never ship before funds clear. Never use an unlicensed exporter.
- Never invent an assay, grade, origin, or compliance claim to close a deal.
- Never skip KYC because the buyer is "in a hurry."
- Never accept payment to a personal account for a company deal.
- Never claim compliance ("conflict-free," "LBMA-standard") you can't document.
