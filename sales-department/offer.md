## What we sell / supply — GOLD

- **Gold doré** — sourced from the Lake Victoria Goldfields (Tanzania, incl. Mwanza
  region) and matched to buyer spec. Form: doré bars `[ASSUMPTION — confirm]`.
- **Gold concentrate** — assayed, spec-confirmed per lot. Grade: per assay

- **Consultation** — sourcing, spec matching, logistics, export/assay documentation,
  and contract structuring for buyers and miners.

## Price

- **Model:** index-linked to **LBMA spot gold**, plus a negotiated premium/discount.
  This is the market-standard pricing basis — not an invented number.
- Exact premium, volume discounts, and payment terms are set per contract.
- **Guiding principle:** every deal clears cost + overhead at a healthy margin.
  Reliability is sold over price — buyers come back for on-spec, on-time delivery.

## Ideal customer (ICP)

| Segment | Who they are | What they need | Why we fit |
|---------|-------------|----------------|------------|
| **Gold refiners** | Smelters/refineries buying doré/concentrate | Reliable, assayed feed supply | We match spec, confirm assay, deliver on schedule |

| **Jewelry / mint / manufacturer buyers** | End-buyers of fine gold | Spec-grade metal, on-schedule | Grade + logistics, spec-confirmed |
| **Investors** | Funds/individuals holding physical gold | Long-term supply lines | Repeat supply, spec'd to their profile |

### Fit signals (for the Qualify agent)
- **Fit:** they buy gold doré/concentrate and we can match their spec + assay.
- **Need:** an active buying program, refinery feed gap, or tender — not vague interest.
- **Budget:** clear volume/term expectations; comfortable with spot-indexed pricing.
- **Timing:** a real buying window or supply contract (not "someday").

## Voice & style (for Outreach / Follow-Up / Close)

- **Tone:** professional, direct, B2B. Confidence without overselling. Technical where

- **Length:** short. Lead with value, ask with specifics. Bullets > paragraphs.
- **Sign-off:** `Best, Hendrixx`
