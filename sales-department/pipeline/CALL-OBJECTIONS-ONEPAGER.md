## Objection → honest response

**1. "What's your price?"**
→ Index-linked to LBMA spot plus a negotiated premium/discount. Exact number depends on
your spec, volume, and term — I'll quote per lot, never a made-up figure.

**2. "Can you match our grade / assay?"**
→ Grade is confirmed by independent fire assay per lot and matched to your spec before
commitment. If we can't hit it, I'll tell you up front.

**3. "Is your gold compliant / documented?"**
→ Mineral dealer licence, export permit, assay certificate, and ICGLR/OECD traceability
on every lot. *(Confirm exactly which docs you actually hold before claiming.)*

**4. "How do I know you're not a scam?"** *(very common in this trade — don't take it personally)*
→ I don't ask you to trust me blind: we do independent assay, proper export permits, and
I'm happy to start with a small trial lot or a sample assay report. Payment terms that
protect you too.

**5. "What's your minimum volume?"**
→ `[Fill in your real minimum]`. If they want smaller, say so honestly and offer a
trial/sample route.

**6. "We already have suppliers."**
→ Good — then this is a benchmark conversation, not a pitch. If our spec/price/logistics
are competitive, we're worth a second source. No pressure either way.

**7. "Payment terms?"**
→ Standard for us is `[fill in]`. Open to aligning with an LC/escrow if the volume and
term justify it.

**8. "Logistics / delivery — how does it get to us?"**
→ We coordinate freight, insurance, and export docs to your destination. Lead time
`[fill in]` from PO. *(Confirm FOB/CIF and which ports.)*

**9. "Timing — not now."**
→ Understood. I'll log you for a specific re-check date if you want; no further nudges
until then. Your call.

**10. "Where's your source / mine?"**
→ Lake Victoria Goldfields — the Geita, Mwanza, Shinyanga, Mara region. Sourced through
licensed channels. *(State your actual sourcing arrangement honestly.)*

## Talk track (the call arc)

1. **Open** — restate what they said they need (their words, not yours).
2. **Probe** — spec/grade, monthly volume, delivery terms, start date, documentation needs.
3. **Confirm fit** — what you can actually supply, against `offer.md` / `PRODUCTS.md`; flag any gap honestly.
4. **Next step** — quote / sample assay / draft contract. Never overpromise.

## 🇹🇿 Tanzania compliance facts (reference — verify with your export agent before quoting on a call)

*From the Mining (Local Content) Regs and BoT Gold Purchase Programme. Useful talking
points; not legal advice — confirm current status before relying on them.*

- **Broker licence** — buy from authorised miners at buying stations, sell to licensed
  dealers. Tanzanian citizens/local companies only. **Cannot export.**
- **Dealer licence** — can buy from miners/brokers and **can export**.
- **Small-scale miners (PML holders)** sell at Mineral & Gemstone Auction Centres (28
  government centres since 2019).
- **BoT Gold Purchase Programme** — gold delivered to designated refineries (Geita /
  Mwanza); LBMA benchmark pricing; **royalty reduced to 4%** (vs 6% standard export);
  **inspection fee waived**; reduced withholding tax.
- **VAT** — zero-rated on domestic gold supply to local refineries/smelters (Finance Act 2024).
- **Designated refineries:** Mwanza Precious Metals Refinery (Mwanza), Eyes of Africa (Dodoma), Geita plant.
