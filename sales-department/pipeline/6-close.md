### Likely objections → honest answers (gold supply)

- **"What's your price?"** → We price index-linked to LBMA spot plus a negotiated
  premium/discount. Exact number depends on your spec, volume, and term — I'll quote
  per lot, never a made-up figure.
- **"Can you match our grade/assay?"** → We confirm grade by independent assay per lot
  and match to your spec before any commitment. If we can't hit it, I'll tell you up
  front.
- **"Is your gold compliant/documented?"** → Yes — mineral dealer licence, export
  permit, assay certificate, and ICGLR/OECD traceability on every lot.
- **"Timing — not now."** → Understood. I'll log you for a specific re-check date if you
  want; no further nudges until then.
- **"What's your minimum volume?"** → `[ASSUMPTION — confirm real min-lot with me before
  answering this on a call]`.

3. **Confirm fit** — what we can actually supply, against `offer.md` / `PRODUCTS.md`;

4. **Next step** — quote / sample assay / draft contract. Never overpromise.
