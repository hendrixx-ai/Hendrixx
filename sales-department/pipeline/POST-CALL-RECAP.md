## Notes to self (delete before sending)

- **Restate their priority, not yours.** If the call revealed a gap (grade/volume we
  can't hit), say so honestly in the recap — don't paper over it.
- **Be specific on dates.** "By Tuesday" beats "soon."
- **If they committed to something** (send docs, intro, decision by X), put it in the
  "You:" line so there's a written record.
